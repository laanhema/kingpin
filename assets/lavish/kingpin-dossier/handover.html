What Is Wrong Today

1. A moved merge stops the whole landing

Problem: Landing merges origin/main into the task branch, then merges the pull request. If main moved between the two steps, the first merge fails, the script writes failed: and stops. In the last two weeks 6 of 41 landings stopped this way, and 5 of the 6 landed fine when a person ran the same command again.

Cost: Each stop waits for you to notice it, read the log and re-run. The wait is the expensive part, not the retry.

In short: 5 of 6 stuck landings would have fixed themselves with one more try. The proposal is to make that try automatic, once, and to tell you right away if it fails again.

How It Would Work

Three Options Compared

Option A

Do nothing

  • Landings that move under us keep stopping
  • You re-run them by hand
  • No code to maintain
Option B RECOMMENDED

Retry once, then ask

  • One retry after fetching main
  • A second failure becomes needs-input:
  • Smallest change that fixes 5 of 6 cases
Option C

Retry with backoff and a queue

  • Up to 3 retries with a growing wait
  • A queue file so retries survive a restart
  • Covers rarer cases, adds config to maintain

Recommendation: Option B. It fixes the common case with one small loop, tells you at once when the retry fails, and is easy to revert if it turns out to hide something.

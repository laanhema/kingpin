What Changed and Why

1. A stop hook in bin/example-stop

Problem: A worker that finished or died without writing a status line left a window that looked busy. The supervisor only noticed later, as a quiet pane.

Solution: One helper now runs on every stop. It decides whether the turn ended with a report and, if not, appends exactly one status line. A status written during the current turn is never touched.

2. Tests for the three stop cases

Problem: Nothing exercised a silent stop, a waiting stop or an already-reported stop.

Solution: A new suite drives each case with a fake worker and asserts the one status line that results.
